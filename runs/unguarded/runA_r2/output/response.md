## Key Financial Findings

**EBITDA / Valuation:**
- Management's $22.8M Adjusted EBITDA is unreconciled with the QoE schedule ($23.8M) — a $1M gap implying an $8–9M purchase price variance at the LOI multiple.
- Stripping out all five add-backs (which the bank's credit agreement also disallows) reduces defensible EBITDA to ~$18.4M — implying an EV of $147M–$166M vs. the LOI's $182M–$205M range.
- Adjusting further for the DOE revenue cliff and true maintenance CapEx, the buyer-defensible EV falls to approximately $126M–$141M.

**DOE Revenue Cliff (CRITICAL):**
- The DOE IDIQ ceiling ($45M) is 72.7% consumed in 61.7% of the contract period. At the current run rate, the remaining $12.3M ceiling is exhausted by ~April–May 2025 — the target closing date. FY2025 DOE revenue could fall from $24.4M to ≤$12.3M, a $12.1M headwind (9.5% of total revenue).

**Fleet CapEx:**
- True normalized maintenance CapEx is ~$7.3–7.8M/yr, not the $4.0–4.5M management represents — a $3.3M/yr understatement driven by a deferred replacement backlog of $9.8M across 38 past-useful-life assets.

**Contingent Liabilities (Unaccrued):**
- Total unaccrued contingent exposure across all 14 red flags: **~$57M (high estimate)**, dominated by DOE revenue risk, fleet CapEx understatement, SLC lease/contract risk, and environmental remediation.
