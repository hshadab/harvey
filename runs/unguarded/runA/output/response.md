## Red Flag Memo (`red-flag-memo.docx`)

A 22-issue memorandum organized into six risk categories with full narrative analysis, source citations, and recommended actions for each finding.

**Three Critical / Highest-Priority Issues:**

1. **DOE IDIQ Ceiling Exhaustion (RF-12 — CRITICAL):** The Company's largest customer (19.2% of revenue, $24.4M) will exhaust its $45M IDIQ contract ceiling by April–May 2025 — at or before closing — creating a **$12.1M+ FY2025 revenue headwind** that is not reflected in the CIM or valuation.

2. **EBITDA Overstatement (RF-01, RF-02 — HIGH):** A $1.0M unreconciled gap between the CIM ($22.8M) and QoE data ($23.8M), plus $2.5–$3.5M in questionable add-backs (recurring integration costs, ongoing ERP, a litigation *receipt* misclassified as an add-back), suggests sustainable Adjusted EBITDA of **$19.5–$21.0M** vs. the $22.8M valuation basis — implying an EV of ~$156–$189M vs. the $182–$205M indicative range.

3. **Salt Lake City Dual Default Risk (RF-17, RF-10 — HIGH):** The SLC transfer station (~$16.2M revenue, 12.7% of total) operates under an unresolved lease assignment (consent requested April 2022, never received) and an untransferred environmental permit (Pinnacle's name, 2.5+ years post-acquisition). The landlord sent an inquiry in November 2024 suggesting awareness of the issue.
