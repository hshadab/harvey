## Top 6 Critical Issues

### 1. DOE IDIQ Ceiling Exhaustion (~$12.1M FY2025 Revenue Cliff)
The DOE contract ($24.4M, 19.2% of revenue) has only **$12.3M of ceiling remaining** against a $24.4M/year run rate. The ceiling will be exhausted by ~April–May 2025 — coinciding with the target closing date. At 8–9× multiple, this represents **$97–109M of enterprise value at risk** from a single issue.

### 2. Unreconciled EBITDA Discrepancy ($1.0M Gap)
The CIM states Adjusted EBITDA of $22.8M; the QoE data package shows $23.8M. The $1.0M gap is unacknowledged in the CIM and unresolved in the data room. At 8–9×, this implies **$8–9M of EV at risk**.

### 3. Stale Phase II ESA — Uninsured Contamination at HQ
The 2019 Phase II ESA (expressly recommended for update before any transaction) confirmed PCE, TCE, and petroleum hydrocarbon contamination in soil and groundwater at the Grand Junction HQ. The Pollution Legal Liability policy **explicitly excludes gradual contamination** — meaning the full remediation cost ($1.8–2.6M per 2019 estimates, likely higher today) is **uninsured**. Vapor intrusion into an occupied maintenance building has never been assessed.

### 4. Salt Lake City Facility — Dual Unresolved Issues
The SLC transfer station lease assignment (Pinnacle → RES) has been pending **2.5+ years** without landlord consent. The hazardous waste permit (UTH-0441) also remains in Pinnacle's name. The proposed Sycamore acquisition is a **second change-of-control event** on top of the unresolved first. This facility serves **~$16.2M of annual revenue** (12.7% of total).

### 5. NLRB Union Election Petition — Material Omission
A Teamsters Local 455 NLRB election petition covering **62 field technicians** at Grand Junction and Casper was filed November 4, 2024. It is disclosed only in a **footnote** of the benefits summary — absent from the CIM, litigation summary, and all other primary documents. This is a material omission from the seller's disclosure.

### 6. Asbestos Long-Tail with Insurance Gap
The CGL policy has a **blanket asbestos exclusion**. The PLL policy's retroactive date of January 1, 2015 leaves **three years of asbestos abatement work (2012–2014) entirely uninsured**. No tail coverage has been purchased. Two workers' comp claims from former asbestos employees remain open.
