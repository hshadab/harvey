## Key Financial Takeaways

- **Valuation gap**: Buyer-adjusted EBITDA of $19.5M–$21.0M implies EV of $156M–$189M vs. management's $182M–$205M range — a potential $16M–$49M reduction.
- **Revenue sustainability**: DOE revenue ($24.4M, 19.2% of total) faces a ceiling exhaustion cliff by April–May 2025; FY2025 DOE revenue could fall to $12.3M or less.
- **Contingent liabilities**: Total unreserved contingent exposure (excluding mandatory debt repayment of ~$39.2M) ranges from approximately $15M to $46M across all identified items.
- **Certain closing costs**: $2.625M single-trigger severance + ~$39.2M debt repayment = ~$41.8M in certain cash outflows at closing.
