## Key Findings at a Glance

**Valuation Integrity:** The $22.8M Adjusted EBITDA anchor is unreliable — a $1.0M unexplained gap exists between the CIM and the QoE data package, and $5.4M in add-backs are largely disallowed under the bank's credit agreement definition. Using reported EBITDA of $18.4M at the stated 8–9x multiple implies an EV of $147–$166M vs. the $182–$205M indicative range.

**Revenue Cliff:** The DOE IDIQ contract (19.2% of revenue) will exhaust its $45M ceiling by approximately April–May 2025 — the target closing date — creating a potential $12.1M revenue headwind in FY2025 that is not disclosed in the CIM.

**Environmental:** The owned Grand Junction headquarters has documented soil and groundwater contamination (PCE, TCE, petroleum hydrocarbons) assessed in 2019 at $1.8–2.6M — but the assessment is stale, vapor intrusion at the occupied maintenance building has never been evaluated, and off-site plume migration is unconfirmed. The 2019 cost estimate explicitly excludes these items.

**Salt Lake City Nexus:** The SLC transfer station has three simultaneous unresolved issues: (1) a 32-month-old unanswered lease assignment request, (2) a hazardous waste permit still in the name of a dormant subsidiary, and (3) ~$16.2M in annual revenue dependent on the facility. The landlord sent an inquiry in November 2024 signaling awareness.

**Hidden Disclosure:** The NLRB Teamsters election petition (filed November 4, 2024, covering 62 field technicians at the two largest facilities) appears only in a footnote of the Employee Benefits Summary — it is not disclosed anywhere in the CIM.
