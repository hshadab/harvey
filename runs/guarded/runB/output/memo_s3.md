## SECTION III — DEBT AND CREDIT AGREEMENT

### RF-13 | CRITICAL | Change-of-Control Triggers Mandatory Full Debt Payoff

The credit agreement contains a Change of Control provision triggered by the proposed stock acquisition. Upon a Change of Control, the Borrower must prepay all outstanding Obligations within five business days. Total estimated mandatory prepayment is approximately $39 million. The credit agreement provides no waiver or consent mechanism.

**Action:** Confirm that Buyer's acquisition financing includes sufficient proceeds to repay the existing credit facility at closing. Obtain a payoff letter from the lender.

### RF-14 | HIGH | Bank EBITDA vs. Management EBITDA — Material Gap

The bank's Consolidated EBITDA definition permits only a limited amount of non-recurring add-backs, yielding a figure materially lower than management's Adjusted EBITDA for covenant compliance purposes.

**Action:** Obtain copies of all compliance certificates delivered to the lender for the past four quarters.

### RF-15 | HIGH | CEO Key-Man Covenant — Event of Default if He Departs

The credit agreement requires the CEO to remain in his role. His departure without a Lender-approved successor constitutes an Event of Default with no cure period.

**Action:** Confirm that the credit facility will be fully repaid at closing. Negotiate a post-closing retention arrangement with the CEO.
