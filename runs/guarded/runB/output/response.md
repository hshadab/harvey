## The Six Critical Issues

**RF-01 — EBITDA Discrepancy ($1.0M gap, $8–9M valuation impact)**
The CIM and QoE data package present different Adjusted EBITDA figures for FY2024. The QoE package itself acknowledges the gap is unreconciled. At 8.0x–9.0x, this implies a valuation swing of $8–9M. Neither figure can be relied upon until reconciled.

**RF-02 — DOE IDIQ Ceiling Exhaustion (~$12M revenue cliff in FY2025)**
The Company's largest customer relationship (19.2% of TTM revenue) is an IDIQ contract with a fixed ceiling. At the current run rate, the remaining ceiling will be exhausted by approximately the target closing date, leaving ~16 months of unfunded contract period. Potential ~$12M revenue headwind in FY2025 — not disclosed in the CIM's investment highlights.

**RF-08 — Stale Phase II ESA, Vapor Intrusion Unassessed**
The Phase II ESA for the Company's only owned property is 5+ years old. It identified significant soil and groundwater contamination and explicitly recommended a vapor intrusion assessment for the occupied vehicle maintenance building — which has never been conducted. The contamination is characterized as "gradual release," meaning it is explicitly excluded from the Pollution Legal Liability policy. The Company has no insurance coverage for this liability.

**RF-13 — Mandatory ~$39M Debt Payoff Within 5 Business Days of Closing**
The credit agreement's Change of Control provision is triggered by the proposed stock acquisition. The Borrower must prepay all outstanding Obligations within five business days. No waiver or consent mechanism exists. Total estimated mandatory prepayment: ~$39.2–39.3M.

**RF-16 — Salt Lake City Lease — Unresolved Assignment, Dual Change-of-Control Risk**
The Salt Lake City transfer station lease (serving ~$16M of annual revenue) has not received landlord consent to assignment despite a request filed 32+ months ago. The landlord sent an inquiry in November 2024 referencing the consent-to-assignment clause. The proposed acquisition would constitute a second change-of-control event requiring a separate consent.

**RF-22 — DOE Contract Non-Assignable, Novation May Be Required**
The DOE IDIQ contract is non-assignable without prior written consent of the Contracting Officer. The contract requires written notification within 30 calendar days of any change in ownership, with failure to notify being grounds for termination for default.
