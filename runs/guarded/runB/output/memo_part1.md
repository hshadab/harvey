# DILIGENCE RED FLAG MEMORANDUM
## Project Ridgeline — Acquisition of Ridgeline Environmental Services, Inc.

**Prepared for:** Sycamore Capital Partners, LP / Thornfield & Associates LLP  
**Date:** December 2024  
**Subject:** Red Flag Analysis — All Data Room Documents Reviewed

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Thirty-one red flags were identified across nine risk categories after reviewing all thirteen data room documents for the proposed acquisition of Ridgeline Environmental Services, Inc. at an indicative enterprise value of approximately $182–$205 million. Eleven issues are rated **Critical** (requiring resolution prior to or as a condition of closing), fourteen are rated **High** (requiring specific indemnification, price adjustment, or pre-closing remediation), and six are rated **Medium** (warranting enhanced representations, warranties, and post-closing monitoring).

The five most urgent issues requiring resolution before signing are: (1) an unexplained discrepancy between the CIM and QoE Adjusted EBITDA figures; (2) a projected major DOE revenue shortfall from IDIQ ceiling exhaustion coinciding with the target closing date; (3) an unresolved Salt Lake City lease assignment creating a pre-existing default risk affecting a significant portion of annual revenue; (4) a stale Phase II ESA with unassessed vapor intrusion risk at the Company's only owned property; and (5) a mandatory full debt payoff triggered by the change-of-control provision.
