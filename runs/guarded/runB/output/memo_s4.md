## SECTION IV — REAL PROPERTY AND LEASES

### RF-16 | CRITICAL | Salt Lake City Lease — Unresolved Assignment, Dual Change-of-Control Risk

The Salt Lake City transfer station lease was originally executed by the acquired subsidiary. RES requested landlord consent to assignment in April 2022. As of December 2024 — approximately 32 months later — the landlord has not provided written consent. In November 2024, the landlord sent correspondence inquiring about the current tenant of record. The proposed acquisition would constitute a second change-of-control event under the lease.

**Action:** Obtain written landlord consent to both the existing assignment and the proposed acquisition as a condition of closing.

### RF-17 | MEDIUM | Pueblo Storage Yard Lease Expiring at Closing

The Pueblo, Colorado storage yard lease expires on the same date as the target closing. Renewal negotiations are underway but no renewal has been executed.

**Action:** Require execution of a lease renewal or alternative arrangement as a condition of closing.
