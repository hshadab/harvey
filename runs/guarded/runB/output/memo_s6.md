## SECTION VI — GOVERNMENT CONTRACTS

### RF-22 | CRITICAL | DOE Contract — Non-Assignable, Novation May Be Required

The Company's largest government contract is non-assignable without prior written consent of the Contracting Officer pursuant to the Anti-Assignment Act and applicable federal acquisition regulations. The contract requires written notification to the Contracting Officer within 30 calendar days of any change in ownership or control, with failure to notify being grounds for termination for default.

**Action:** Engage directly with the DOE Contracting Officer before closing to determine whether a novation agreement is required. Make government notification a condition of closing.

### RF-23 | HIGH | Multiple Government Contracts with Change-of-Control Notification Requirements

Eight of the top twenty customer contracts contain assignment, change-of-control, or notification provisions, collectively representing approximately $30 million or more of annual revenue.

**Action:** Develop a comprehensive consent and notification matrix for all affected contracts. Prioritize obtaining pre-closing consents from the most critical relationships.
