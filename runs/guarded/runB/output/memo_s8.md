## SECTION VIII — INSURANCE

### RF-27 | HIGH | Pollution Legal Liability — Gradual Contamination Exclusion Covers Owned Property

The Pollution Legal Liability policy explicitly excludes coverage for gradual contamination at owned or operated facilities. The Phase II ESA characterizes the Grand Junction contamination as resulting from gradual releases over multiple decades. The Company has no insurance coverage for the primary environmental liability at its only owned property.

**Action:** Negotiate a specific environmental indemnification from sellers for the Grand Junction remediation liability with a six-year survival period. Evaluate procurement of a historical contamination endorsement.

### RF-28 | HIGH | Claims-Made PLL Policy — Tail Coverage Risk at Change of Control

The Pollution Legal Liability policy is written on a claims-made basis with a retroactive date of January 1, 2015. At a change of control, the policy may not automatically transfer to the new owner. An extended reporting period (tail) is available at 150% of the expiring annual premium for a 36-month extension.

**Action:** Confirm with the insurer the impact of the proposed transaction on the PLL policy. Procure tail coverage as part of the closing process.
