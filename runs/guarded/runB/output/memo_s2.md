## SECTION II — ENVIRONMENTAL

### RF-08 | CRITICAL | Phase II ESA — Stale (5+ Years), Vapor Intrusion Unassessed, Cost Estimate Outdated

The Phase II Environmental Site Assessment for the Company's only owned property (the Grand Junction headquarters) was completed in June 2019 — over five years ago. It identified petroleum hydrocarbon contamination and chlorinated solvent contamination in soil and groundwater at concentrations exceeding applicable regulatory standards. Elevated soil gas concentrations adjacent to the occupied vehicle maintenance building indicate a potential vapor intrusion pathway — a vapor intrusion assessment was explicitly recommended as a matter warranting prompt attention but has never been conducted. The 2019 cost estimate has not been updated and excludes vapor intrusion mitigation, off-site remediation (no off-site sentinel wells have been installed), and inflation adjustments. The contamination is characterized as resulting from gradual releases over multiple decades, meaning it is explicitly excluded from the Pollution Legal Liability policy.

**Action:** Commission an updated Phase II ESA and vapor intrusion assessment immediately. Obtain an independent remediation cost estimate in current dollars. Negotiate a specific environmental escrow or indemnification with a six-year survival period. Require seller to initiate voluntary cleanup program enrollment before closing.

### RF-09 | HIGH | CDPHE RCRA Enforcement Action — Unaccrued Penalty Exposure

CDPHE issued a Notice of Violation in July 2024 alleging improper storage of listed hazardous waste at the Grand Junction facility. The parties dispute the violation period, with the Company's position implying a maximum penalty of approximately $450K and CDPHE's position implying a maximum penalty of approximately $1.16 million. No accrual has been recorded as of the fiscal year end. The matter is in administrative negotiation with a settlement conference expected in early 2025 — potentially after the target signing date.

**Action:** Require resolution before closing, or establish a specific indemnification escrow. Confirm the enforcement action will not affect the upcoming permit renewal.

### RF-10 | HIGH | Salt Lake City Permit — Transfer Pending Since March 2022

The Utah hazardous waste transfer station permit for the Salt Lake City facility remains in the name of the acquired subsidiary despite the acquisition closing in March 2022. A transfer application was filed but has not been completed after more than two years. The proposed acquisition would layer a second change-of-control event on top of the unresolved first transfer. The Salt Lake City facility serves customers generating approximately $16 million of annual revenue.

**Action:** Obtain written confirmation from the Utah Division of Waste Management that current operations are compliant. Make completion of the permit transfer a condition of closing.

---

### RF-11 | HIGH | Wyoming Permit — Expired, Operating Under Timely Renewal

The Wyoming hazardous waste permit for the Casper facility expired in late November 2024. A renewal application was filed in October 2024. The Company is operating under timely renewal provisions, which do not guarantee renewal. An expired permit is also an Event of Default under the credit agreement. The Casper facility serves approximately 24% of total revenue.

**Action:** Obtain written confirmation from Wyoming DEQ of the expected renewal timeline. Make permit renewal a condition of closing.

### RF-12 | MEDIUM | Asbestos Abatement Long-Tail Liability — Uninsured Pre-2015 Exposure

The Company operated an asbestos abatement service line from 2012 through September 2023, performing work at approximately 145 commercial and industrial sites. The current Pollution Legal Liability policy has a retroactive date of January 1, 2015, leaving work performed from 2012–2014 without insurance coverage for future claims. The CGL policy contains a blanket asbestos exclusion with no tail coverage procured. Two workers' compensation claims from former asbestos abatement employees remain open.

**Action:** Obtain a specific asbestos indemnification from sellers with a survival period matching the applicable statute of limitations. Evaluate procurement of a tail policy covering pre-2015 operations.
